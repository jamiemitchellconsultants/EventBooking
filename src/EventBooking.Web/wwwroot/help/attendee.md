# Attendee guide

Everything on this page happens through the links emailed to you. There is no account to create and nothing to sign in to — each link is single-use and only works for you, so please do not forward them.

## Choose a time

Your invitation email holds a booking link. Opening it shows the times currently open for your appointment and the locations they are at. Pick one and confirm it. If the time you wanted has just filled up, it is removed from the list and you can choose another straight away.

Your confirmation email follows shortly afterwards and holds your management link. Keep it: it is the only way back to your booking.

## Manage

Your management link shows your appointment and lets you cancel it. There are two ways to cancel:

- Cancel booking releases your place. The recruitment team follows up about what happens next.
- Cancel and choose a new time releases your place and emails you a fresh invitation with the times still open.

If a replacement invitation is already on its way, you are pointed back at that link instead of being sent a duplicate. If nothing is available right now, the team will be in touch when another time opens up.

## When a link stops working

Links expire, and a booking link can only be used once. An expired page says so plainly — contact your coordinator and they will send a fresh one. If your appointment has already started, the management link explains that it can no longer be cancelled there and gives you the contact to reach instead.
