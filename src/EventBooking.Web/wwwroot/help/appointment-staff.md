# Appointment staff guide

Appointment staff run the day itself: checking attendees in as they arrive and recording what happened to each appointment.

## Appointment workspace

The appointment workspace lists the confirmed events you are staffing. Opening one shows its roster: every booked attendee, the appointment types they are attending for, and each appointment's current status.

## Check in

Check in each attendee as they arrive. Checking in records the time and moves the appointment from expected to checked in. If the attendee never arrives, leave them expected until the event is over rather than checking them in speculatively.

## Complete

Complete each appointment once it has happened. Completing records the outcome and counts the appointment towards the attendee's readiness — it is the step that moves them closer to having nothing outstanding.

## No-show

Mark a no-show for attendees who neither arrived nor cancelled. A no-show keeps the appointment outstanding on the attendee's record, so the coordinator can arrange a new time for the missed appointment through recovery.

## Correcting mistakes

Outcomes recorded in error can be corrected: a wrong completion or no-show can be set back so the appointment can be recorded properly. Corrections are themselves audited, so the record shows what changed and when.
